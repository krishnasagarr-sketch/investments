Scan your mailboxes for statements and documents

Reads the Gmail inbox already set up on the Notifications tab over IMAP (same App Password, used to read instead of send) — plus the mailbox of every bank account that has an email and app password saved — and keeps the attachments (PDF, CSV, Excel) of emails from a recognised sender: your banks, the Income Tax Department, NPS / EPF record-keepers and anyone you add. Each is filed under Saved attachments below with its document date, the password sentence from the email and the bank account it belongs to, ready to unlock, move to the right record, or — for a deposit receipt — turn into a draft deposit to review. The text of an email is not mined for transactions. Every email looked at is remembered (by its own unique Message-ID), so re-scanning never processes the same mail twice.

Recognised senders

Mail Scan only saves the attachments of emails from senders it recognises. Built in: banks, the Income Tax Department, and NPS / EPF record-keepers. Add anyone else whose statements you want kept — an advisor, a mutual-fund registrar, an insurer. Emails you forward to this mailbox count by their original sender.

Some mail gives no password instruction as text (NPS statements arrive as pictures only). Give its sender a password note once — to set one for a built-in sender such as NPS, add its name (proteantech) here with the note — and every file from it gets that note, which unlocking then reads.
