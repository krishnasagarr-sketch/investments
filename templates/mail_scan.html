Scan your inbox for bank transactions

Reads the Gmail inbox already set up on the Notifications tab over IMAP (same App Password, used to read instead of send) and looks for transaction-looking emails — interest credits and FD-booking confirmations. This is a best-effort keyword scan, not a per-bank parser: bank alert wording varies a lot, so expect it to miss some real emails and occasionally flag something irrelevant. Only the email's own text is scanned for transactions — attachments (e.g. PDF e-statements) aren't read yet. A PDF/CSV/Excel attachment on a bank-looking email is saved to a folder on disk for a future version to work through, but nothing in it is parsed or acted on today. Nothing found here is written to a real record automatically: an interest credit needs a depositor and bank chosen below before it becomes an Interest Check line, and an FD-booking guess lands on the Draft Deposits tab for you to review, edit, and approve — or reject — before it's ever a real deposit. Every email looked at is remembered (by its own unique Message-ID) so re-scanning never processes the same mail twice, and the same transaction is never queued here more than once even if it showed up in more than one email.
