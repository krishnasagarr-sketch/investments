Closed deposits

Deposits that were reinvested or withdrawn — at maturity, or closed early — kept here for the record, but out of the Deposits tab and every current-holdings total (Dashboard, DICGC, Tags). Past interest they earned still counts fully for TDS, the Tax estimate, and the Income & Expenditure statement, for whichever financial year it falls in — closing a deposit doesn't erase history, it just retires it. "Interest/Value at Closure" is frozen as of the actual closed date, not today, so an early closure doesn't look like it kept accruing after it stopped existing.
