Transactions from bank statements

Press Read transactions beside a saved bank-statement PDF on Mail Scan (the email must be sorted into a bank account first) and its lines are listed here with a suggested category. Nothing counts until you press Add: money in becomes an Other Income entry, money out an Expenses entry (so both reach the I&E Statement), FD interest credits go to Interest Check (deposit interest is already counted from the deposits themselves), and anything that isn't income or spending — transfers, FD bookings, investments — is ignored. It can misread a line, so look through the suggestions; a line left on Decide later stays here. Each added line can be undone below.
