TDS on FD interest (Resident)

Banks deduct TDS at {{ tds_rate_pct }}% (with PAN on file — 20% without) when a depositor's total interest from a bank in a financial year crosses the threshold (₹40,000 normally, ₹50,000 for senior citizens). Resident deposits only — NRE and FCNR interest is exempt from tax and TDS entirely, and NRO has its own rules and rate (see below). This is an estimate from what's recorded here, not a substitute for your bank's own TDS certificate (Form 16A).

TDS on NRO interest

NRO interest is taxed at source under Section 195, not the resident rules above: {{ nro_tds_rate_pct }}% flat (30% + 4% health & education cess) from the first rupee — no ₹40,000 threshold. A surcharge may also apply above certain income levels, which isn't included here, so treat this as a floor, not the final figure.
